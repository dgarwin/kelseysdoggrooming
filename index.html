Services

Every visit is tailored to your dog. Not sure what you need? Just ask.

  • Full Groom

    Bath, blow-dry, breed-appropriate or custom haircut, nails, ears, and a finishing brush-out.

  • Bath & Tidy

    Deep clean, thorough dry, sanitary trim, and a tidy of the face, feet, and tail.

  • De-shedding

    Undercoat treatment for double-coated breeds — dramatically less hair on your couch.

  • Nails & Ears

    Quick walk-in style service: nail trim and file, ear cleaning, and a paw pad tidy.

  • Puppy's First Groom

    A short, gentle introduction that builds confidence so grooming stays stress-free for life.

  • Senior & Special Needs

    Patient, low-stress handling with extra breaks for older dogs and anxious pups.

About Kelsey

Kelsey Buckley is a professional dog groomer working across the DMV. She takes a small number of dogs at a time so every one gets real attention — the right products for their coat, the pace they need, and a groomer who remembers their name.

Whether it's a doodle that needs a full reset, a shepherd blowing its coat, or a nervous rescue on their very first visit, the goal is the same: a dog that comes home comfortable and looking great.
