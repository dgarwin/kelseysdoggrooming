About Kelsey

I'm Kelsey Buckley. I studied animal science at Rutgers University and started my career as a zookeeper — which taught me more about reading an animal's body language than any classroom could. Somewhere along the way I realized the animals I most wanted to work with were the ones people bring home.

These days I'm a part-time bather at a corporate grooming salon while I grow my skills as a groomer, and I groom a small number of dogs of my own on the side. Those grooms happen in your home rather than a shop, which spares your dog the car ride, the cage, and the noise of a busy salon — for an anxious dog, that alone changes the whole appointment. Working for free is how I get the reps: no rushing, no assembly line, and the time to do it gently. Nervous dogs, seniors, and first-timers are all welcome.
